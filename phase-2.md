# Phase 2: five to ten homes, the same measurements.

Phase 1 measures one node in one home. That cannot separate what is true of residential compute from what is true of that household. Phase 2 repeats the Phase 1 measurements in five to ten Medicine Hat homes with different energy profiles, using identical loaned hardware. It is a research sample with a fixed end date.

![Phase 2 overview: five to ten homes in three energy profiles, the loaned kit each home receives, the February to June schedule, and what is compared across homes.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homenodes-1/homenodes-framework/main/diagrams/phase-2-overview.png)

*Phase 2 at a glance. Planned design. Phase 2 depends on Phase 1 results.*

### WHAT IT COMPARES

- **Energy use.** How does node energy use compare across household energy profiles?
- **Workload identifiability.** Does the Phase 1 result hold behind different household loads?
- **Governance gaps.** Which gaps repeat across households, and which depend on the home?
- **Household impact.** What does hosting a node do to a household: heat, noise, circuit load?

### THE HOMES

Five to ten households in Medicine Hat, spread across three profiles: gas-primary, solar-supplemented, and grid-primary. The Phase 1 home continues as the reference node.

### THE HARDWARE

Each home receives the same kit on loan: a node built to the Phase 1 specification, a firewall, an energy monitor on the node circuit, and a dedicated internet line. The project owns and operates every node. Households provide space and power, and are reimbursed for the electricity the node uses. All hardware is removed when the study ends.

### THE SCHEDULE

Target dates. They move if Phase 1 moves.

- **February 2027.** Protocol written. Independent ethics review
- **March 2027.** Households recruited. Written consent signed
- **April 2027.** Loaned kit installed. Baseline recorded in every home
- **May to June 2027.** Eight weeks of operation under protocol
- **End of June 2027.** Hardware removed. Results and data published

### CONSENT AND LIMITS

- No household is recruited before independent ethics review is complete
- Written consent, with the right to withdraw at any time
- Data de-identified before publication. No addresses published
- No more than ten homes
- A fixed end date for every node

### WHAT IT COSTS

A planning estimate, revised once Phase 1 shows actual costs.

- Five homes: about CAD $42,400
- Ten homes: about CAD $80,200
- Per home: about CAD $6,900, of which CAD $5,523 is loaned hardware
- Research time: unpaid

### WHAT IT CANNOT SHOW

The sample is small and made up of volunteers in one city. The hardware is identical, which makes homes comparable but is less like real residential hosting. Phase 2 does not test what happens when work is split across many nodes, or how quickly many nodes can be shut down.

## The plan is a draft.

The Phase 2 protocol will be written and independently reviewed before anyone is recruited. The current plan, its open decisions, and the line-by-line estimate are on GitHub.
