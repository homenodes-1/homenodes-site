# Who we are looking to work with.

HomeNodes is an independent research initiative seeking partnerships with organizations that have a direct stake in how residential compute infrastructure gets governed. Active conversations are underway with several potential partners. If your organization belongs on this list, we want to hear from you.

### CITY OF MEDICINE HAT

The city's municipal utility ownership makes it a natural research partner. HomeNodes is seeking a formal research relationship to share energy consumption data, explore zoning implications, and involve city planners and utility managers in governance framework development.

### IEEE COMPUTER SOCIETY

The project aligns with IEEE technical communities in distributed processing, security and privacy, and internet governance. The Emerging Technologies Fund supports work of this kind.

### BLUEDOT IMPACT

BlueDot's focus on compute governance and AI safety infrastructure aligns directly with the project's goals. Its Rapid Grants program supports early-stage work of this kind.

## Interested in partnering?

We are looking for researchers, policymakers, distributed compute platform operators, and municipal governments who want to engage with this work early. The governance framework will be stronger with more perspectives in it.
