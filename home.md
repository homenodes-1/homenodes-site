# AI compute is moving into homes. The rules haven't followed it there yet.

[Read the Research Case](https://homenodes.ca/the-project)

Distributed AI compute is moving into residential homes. The governance frameworks being built today don't account for it.

HomeNodes is a research initiative investigating what residential AI compute nodes mean for oversight, energy policy, and public safety — starting with a proof of concept in Medicine Hat, Alberta, one of Canada's most uniquely positioned cities for this work.

## One city. One home. A governance framework for everywhere.

Medicine Hat, Alberta is one of the few cities in Canada that owns and operates its own electric, natural gas, and water utilities. That makes it the right place to start. The first HomeNodes proof of concept is being built in a single residence here — monitoring real AI compute workloads against a municipally visible energy grid, and documenting every governance gap along the way.

[Why Medicine Hat](https://homenodes.ca/why-medicine-hat)

## The governance gap won't close itself.

HomeNodes is looking for researchers, policymakers, Medicine Hat residents willing to host a node, and funders who understand why this matters now — before residential compute becomes too embedded to govern retroactively.
